VOID SIGHT Support

Offline alien sniper game for iPhone

How to play

  1. Tap the play icon.
  2. Drag the scope and release to fire.
  3. Hit a glowing core to clear an alien.
  4. During a counterattack, avoid the red card and tap a cyan dodge action.

Compatibility

VOID SIGHT requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later and is played in landscape orientation.

Troubleshooting

  • Use SND to mute or restore game audio.
  • Use FX to enable reduced-motion mode.
  • If audio is unavailable, check the device volume and Silent mode.

Contact

Report a problem through GitHub Issues. Include the iPhone model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the problem.
