VOID SIGHT Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

VOID SIGHT is an offline single-player game. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data collection

The app contains no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, social features, or third-party SDKs. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or other personal information.

Network access

The current version does not connect to external servers. Gameplay and sound generation run locally on the device.

Children's privacy

Because no personal information is collected, the app does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at VOID SIGHT Support.
